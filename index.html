PERSONAL FILE · EYES ONLY

To my Raven,

I don't think you fully understand what it means to be the person someone can finally stop being strong around.

You have told me to fight my battles, and you have also made sure I know I can put the fight down and come back to you. You have sat with me when I had no elegant words, no tidy explanation, and no version of myself worth presenting. You didn't ask me to make it pretty. You let me cry. You let me be your kid.

I'm used to being the person people come to. With you, I get to be the person who comes to someone. You ask if I reached home. You ask how my day was. You think of me when you're out and bring me little things. You call me “kid” like the world can wait a minute and I don't have to carry everything by myself.

And I remember what you told me when things were hard: that you don't usually promise forever, but for me, you would stay forever. I don't take that lightly. I hope you know that you never have to earn the place you have in my life by being useful, brave, or okay. You get to be tired here, too.

I'm proud of the woman you are, and of the writer who put two books into the world. But beyond every achievement, I'm proud of your gentleness. Of the way you notice. Of the way you make room.

Happy birthday, akka. I hope this year gives some of that care back to you. I hope someone checks on you, celebrates you, and remembers the small things. I hope you get to be soft without feeling you have to apologize for it.

You are the elder sister I wished for before I knew what to call that wish. My Raven. My safe place. My person.

Always your kid. ♡
